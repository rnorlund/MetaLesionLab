Take the naming test

Take the naming test

Name about 20 pictures out loud, as quickly as you can. It takes about 3 minutes. Afterwards you'll see how many you named and how fast.

Language
  • You need a microphone and a quiet room.
  • Your voice is sent to the Meta Model API to be written down. This site keeps no recording and none of your words.
  • Your results stay on this page and are gone when you close it, unless you choose to add a summary to the reference range.

Not a medical test. Many things affect naming: the language, the pictures, being tired. If you're worried about your memory or language, talk to a doctor.

How to answer: say the name with one word.

Each picture appears on its own. After you answer, tap Next or press the space bar. If you don't know it, say so, or wait: after 20 seconds the test moves on.
