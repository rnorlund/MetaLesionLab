2

Copy a copy, and the idea gets lost.

Two of Meta’s AIs passed a drawing back and forth ten times: one described it, the other drew the description. A beanbag became a croissant, then a window. Its name was the first thing to go.

    {{TH_COPIES}}

Muse Spark described each picture; Muse Image drew each description. Left to right: the start, then copies 2 to 10. Plato called a painting a copy of a copy.

Written words lose what you meant.

“I never said she stole my money” means seven different things, depending on the word you stress. From the written words, Meta’s AI got the meaning {{TH_SPARK_K}} times in {{TH_N91}}, no better than guessing. A simple program listening to the sound got {{TH_READER_K}}.
