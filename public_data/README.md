# public_data

The only folder where data files may be committed. Everything here must be public and licensed for redistribution, with its source and license listed below.

No data from real research participants goes anywhere in this repository. Comparisons with people with aphasia use published, group-level numbers, cited in the code that uses them.

| File | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| `general_text_guard.json` | 150 short everyday sentences (100 English, 50 Spanish) written by Muse Spark (`muse-spark-1.3`) for this project with `scripts/make_guard_text.py`; the general-language guard in "What can I cut?" (`metalesionlab/compress.py`). Model-generated, not from any person or corpus | Apache 2.0, with the repository |
| `language_support.json` | Our own measurements (Oct 26 and 27, 2026; 16 languages): picture sets built with Meta's models and naming checks on speech synthesized with macOS voices. No recordings of people. Method in `docs/languages.md` | Apache 2.0, like the code |
| `plato_concept_spread.json` | Derived statistics only (no drawings) from the Plato's Chair experiment: per-concept spread of Muse Image doodles and of human drawings from the Google Quick, Draw! dataset (Google Creative Lab, https://github.com/googlecreativelab/quickdraw-dataset), measured with Meta DINOv2. See `docs/plato.md`. | Our statistics: Apache 2.0. Quick, Draw! data: CC BY 4.0, attribution in the file |
| `plato_variety.json` | Derived statistics only (no drawings, embeddings or per-picture labels) from "Can you prompt it out of the Platonic chair?": per-concept DINOv2 spread of Muse Image doodles under four prompts (usual, "any kind", "an unusual kind", one of each kind Muse Spark listed) and of SDXL under three, against human drawings from the Google Quick, Draw! dataset (Google Creative Lab, https://github.com/googlecreativelab/quickdraw-dataset); kind diversity from Muse Glimmer's multiple-choice labels, and the labeler's validation. See `docs/plato.md`. | Our statistics: Apache 2.0. Quick, Draw! data: CC BY 4.0, attribution in the file |
| `plato_glimmer_inside.json` | Derived statistics only (no pictures, embeddings or distances) from "Plato's Chair inside Glimmer": per concept, the spread of Muse Image doodles and of human drawings from the Google Quick, Draw! dataset (Google Creative Lab, https://github.com/googlecreativelab/quickdraw-dataset) at several stages inside Muse Glimmer 30B, the layer where Muse's doodles collapse, and naming accuracy. Written by `scripts/plato_glimmer_inside_export.py`; see `docs/plato_glimmer.md`. Read by Ask Socrates | Our statistics: Apache 2.0. Quick, Draw! data: CC BY 4.0, attribution in the file |
| `plato_passages.json` | 16 short passages of Plato (Republic, Cratylus, Phaedrus, Meno) in Benjamin Jowett's translation (1892), cut word for word from the Project Gutenberg texts named in each entry (`source_url`), with Stephanus references. `scripts/verify_plato_passages.py` checks them against Gutenberg. The only text Ask Socrates may quote Plato from | Public domain (Jowett's translation); excerpts only, without Gutenberg's header or trademark |
| `plato_languages.json` | Group-level statistics (DINOv2 spread, group share, chance-corrected centroid distances) from our Muse Image doodles and from Quick, Draw! drawings by Google (https://quickdraw.withgoogle.com/data). No drawings or embeddings. See `docs/plato_languages.md` | Derived from Quick, Draw! (CC BY 4.0, attribution in the file) and our own Muse Image outputs |
| `human_naming_profiles.json` | Published group-level numbers from Schwartz et al. 2012, Walker et al. 2018, Yang et al. 2026 (C-STAR group means, with permission), Dell et al. 2007, Roach et al. 1996 and Walker et al. 2011 (citations, DOIs and tables inside the file) | Facts quoted from published papers, with citation |
| `naming_norms_<set>.json` (once written) | Reference range for the public naming test, aggregated by `scripts/aggregate_norms.py` from opt-in summaries of adult volunteers (no audio, words, names or IP addresses); written only from 10 contributors | Collected for this project; summary statistics only |
